# Synthetic data card

All records and documents are invented examples for local development. They do not describe real employees, policies, devices, service state, or support tickets.

`app.py` seeds five short internal documents, two employee rows, two sample devices, a service-state row per tenant, and three tickets. The router data contains 18 manually authored utterance/strategy labels. The labels demonstrate the mechanics of a fitted classifier and do not represent observed user behavior. The split is deterministic and stratified by class; there are only eight held-out examples. The experiment report is generated by `python experiments/evaluate.py`.

Known limitations: hand-authored templates create distribution and wording bias; some strategies are inherently ambiguous; there are no human relevance judgments, production traffic, demographic fields, or external validation. Do not use these data to estimate field performance.


The retrieval experiment uses six manually authored queries with manually assigned relevant seed-document IDs in experiments/retrieval_eval.jsonl. These labels are synthetic judgments from the project author, not independent annotations. Scores are only a pipeline smoke measurement and are not evidence of field performance.

The confidence experiment at `experiments/confidence_eval.jsonl` contains 18 authored synthetic support scenarios with explicit expected response status, evidence kinds, and answer fragments. A fixed 12/6 split separates fitting/threshold selection from the reported holdout. The checks are narrow executable assertions, not independent human assessments of semantic answer correctness; they must not be treated as production calibration data.
